# Changelog

## [0.6.0] - 2026-09-15

Lean public release.

### Changed
- Consolidated the project into a smaller public-facing repository.
- English is the canonical specification; Chinese is the official companion translation.
- Consolidated quality/factuality/innovation rules into one rubric.
- Consolidated per-paper provenance, contribution mapping, version locks, and hallucination traps into one benchmark metadata file.
- Consolidated validation into one script.
- Removed historical self-test artifacts, duplicate examples, temporary regression files, and internal development-only files.

### Preserved
- 12 audited DDI Full Gold Notes.
- DDI, LLM/Agent, and AI4Science profiles.
- Chinese and English output templates.
- DDI Dataset Registry with no-autofill policy.
- Source-safety, contribution-fidelity, evidence-grounding, and anti-hallucination rules.
